Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? We have answers.

How do pre-orders work?

You design a product and set a minimum goal (e.g., 50 orders). Your audience can then pre-order it by authorizing a payment on their card. We only charge their cards and process the payments *after* you manually confirm the campaign is successful. If you don't hit your goal, no one is charged.

How do I get paid?

We use Stripe Connect for secure and reliable payouts. During onboarding, you'll create a Stripe Express account, which links directly to your bank. When you finalize a successful campaign, funds are automatically captured from your customers and transferred to your Stripe balance, minus our platform fee and Stripe's standard processing fees.

Do I have to handle manufacturing and shipping?

PageFairy is a platform for validating demand and processing payments. We don't handle physical production or fulfillment. Our model works best with Print-on-Demand (POD) suppliers. Once your campaign is successful, you'll have a list of orders that you can then fulfill through the POD service of your choice.

What does "zero risk" mean?

Zero financial risk. Because you don't place any manufacturing orders until you have confirmed, paid pre-orders, you never have to spend your own money on inventory that might not sell. Your only investment is your time and creativity.
