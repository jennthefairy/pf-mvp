FAQ

Do customers get charged right away?

No — we only place an authorization hold. Cards are captured if your campaign meets its goal.

Can I connect my own domain?

Yes. Public pages work out of the box; custom domains are available on paid plans.

Is email marketing included?

Yes — FairyFlow provides simple automations and social scheduling integrated with your campaigns.
